Year 9 Geography revision cards

Globalisation:

Define globalisation.
The increasing global connectivity of the world especially by large companies producing and trading goods in many different countries
Define food miles.
The distance food is transported from the time of its making until it reaches the consumer
What is a TNC?
Transnational corporations are companies that operate in at least two countries.
What has globalisation allowed TNCs to do?
To grow because improved technology and transport means products and raw resources can move quickly around the world.
Give 3 advantages of global fashion.
Cheap for consumers; investments in LICs and NICs; more jobs in warehouses and factories.
Give 3 disadvantages of global fashion.
Child labour; poor working conditions; farmers are paid low prices for crops like cotton.
What does PEAL stand for?
P - make your point; E - provide evidence; A - analyse the question; L - link back to your original point.
